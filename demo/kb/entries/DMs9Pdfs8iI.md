## Summary

Uploading the PDF and asking for answers with page numbers lets you jump straight to the source to check.

## The technique

Ask your question and tell the model to quote and cite the page for every claim.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Upload the PDF
2. Ask your question and add: quote the passage and give the page number
3. Open the cited pages to confirm

## Prerequisites

- A chat app that accepts PDFs

## When this is useful

For reports, papers and contracts where you need one detail.

## Caveats

- Always check the cited page: models can still misquote
