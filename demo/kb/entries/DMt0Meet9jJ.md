## Summary

A transcript plus a short prompt turns a meeting recording into decisions, owners and next steps in a minute.

## The technique

Transcribe the recording, then ask for decisions, action items with owners, and open questions.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Record the meeting (with everyone's consent)
2. Transcribe it with Whisper
3. Paste the transcript and ask for decisions, action items with owners, and open questions
4. Send the summary to the attendees

## Prerequisites

- Permission to record the meeting

## When this is useful

After any meeting that ends with things to do.

## Caveats

- Check owners and deadlines: the model can attribute them to the wrong person
